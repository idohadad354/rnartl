Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

This policy explains how RNA Apps ("we") handles information in connection with RNA RTL, an app for Atlassian Confluence Cloud distributed through the Atlassian Marketplace.

Summary

RNA RTL runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform, inside your Confluence site. It has no servers, database, analytics or tracking of its own, and we do not receive the content you write with it.

Content you create

  • Text and formatting in an RTL Block are saved by Confluence inside the page (as the macro body and macro settings). They are stored, backed up and deleted by Atlassian as part of your Confluence data, under your agreement with Atlassian.
  • The app does not send this content anywhere and does not use Forge storage or any external service.
  • The app requests no Atlassian API permissions (OAuth scopes).

Images inserted by URL

If an author inserts an image by web address, each reader's browser loads that image directly from the website that hosts it. That website receives the standard information a browser sends with any request (such as IP address and browser type) and is governed by its own privacy policy. We are not involved in that request.

Information from Atlassian about your subscription

Under the Atlassian Marketplace terms, Atlassian may share information about your subscription with us, such as the Confluence site address, license tier and status, and the name and email address of your technical or billing contact. We use it only to administer licenses, provide support, and send essential service or security notices. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

Support requests

When you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send (such as your name, email address, message and any attachments), through our email provider. We use this information only to answer your request, and we delete it on request or within 24 months after the request is closed.

Cookies

RNA RTL sets no cookies and uses no local tracking. Atlassian's own cookies apply to Confluence and the Marketplace.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Israel's Protection of Privacy Law), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or to object to its use. Email ops@rna-app.com and we will respond within 30 days. Content in your Confluence pages is controlled by your Confluence administrators.

Uninstalling

Uninstalling the app does not delete content from your pages; it stays in your Confluence site under your control.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, notify customers through the Marketplace contact we have on record.
